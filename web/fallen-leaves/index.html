Fallen Leaves

A game for two. Gather one kind of leaf in all four colours before your opponent does.

Opponent

How to play

There are ten kinds of leaf (oak, chestnut, ginkgo, elm, willow, maple, aspen, hawthorn, ivy and ash), each in four colours: green, yellow, orange and red. Hold one kind of leaf in all four colours at the end of your turn and you win.

The table has five rows. The top row is your opponent's leaves, the bottom row is yours, and the three between are the ground. The piles are the column on the left, one for each colour; the rims beside each pile are the leaves still beneath it.

Gather. Drag a face-up leaf from a pile to your row (or just tap it). You may keep only one leaf of each colour, so if you now hold two, drag either one to a glowing place on the ground. A leaf goes at the left or right end of a row, and a column must be full before the next one starts.

Sweep. If a leaf on the ground row nearest you is the same kind as one you hold, tap it to sweep a diagonal rising from it. Its three leaves come up into your hand. Put three back, in any order, from everything you now hold; the sweep is made when the third goes down. You must end with one of each colour. Put them back as they were to change your mind.

Your opponent sits opposite, so their near row is the top row of the ground. You may also pass.
